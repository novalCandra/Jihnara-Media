## Brand & Style
This design system merges the physical delight and friendly tactility of high-engagement gamified consumer apps with the sleek precision of an elite digital creative powerhouse. The brand personality balances hyper-vibrant energy with dark-mode luxury—approachable yet premium, mischievous yet structurally immaculate.

The design movement blends **Tactile Neo-Skeuomorphism** with **Cyber-Glassmorphism**:
- 3D tactile push-states, extrusion ridges, and bouncy interactions inspired by toy-like physical buttons.
- Deep space backgrounds illuminated by multi-stop neon ambient blooms and high-contrast dual-tone gradients.
- Ultra-rounded container architecture that prevents technical sterility, inviting touch, exploration, and sustained visual delight.

## Colors
The palette is built upon high-contrast dynamic polarity: ink-black depths punctuated by hyper-saturated luminescent accents.

- **Canvas & Layering**:
  - `canvas-base`: `#090B0F` (ultra-deep cosmic black, preventing washed-out gray casts)
  - `surface-dim`: `#121620` (subtle elevated container foundation)
  - `surface-bright`: `#1A1F2C` (card highlight layer, active states, and structural overlays)
  - `surface-elevated`: `#242B3D` (floating tooltips, popovers, and sticky modules)
- **Vibrant Spectrum**:
  - `primary-cyan`: `#00D9E8` (electric focal points, key interactions, success micro-states)
  - `primary-cyan-shade`: `#009FA9` (3D extrusion ledge for cyan buttons)
  - `secondary-magenta`: `#EC168F` (high-voltage CTA, urgent interactions, brand badges)
  - `secondary-magenta-shade`: `#A30F63` (3D extrusion ledge for magenta buttons)
  - `tertiary-purple`: `#8B35D8` (transitional color bridges, ambient halos, secondary tags)
  - `tertiary-purple-shade`: `#5F2197` (3D extrusion ledge for purple components)
- **Text & Light Accents**:
  - `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `text-secondary`: `#A0AEC0`
  - `text-tertiary`: `#64748B`
  - `accent-light`: `#F7F7FA` (reserved for high-contrast inverted pill tags or sticker backgrounds)
- **Gradients & Glows**:
  - `gradient-hyper`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #00D9E8 0%, #EC168F 100%)`
  - `gradient-aurora`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #8B35D8 0%, #EC168F 100%)`
  - `gradient-dark-glass`: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(26, 31, 44, 0.7) 0%, rgba(18, 22, 32, 0.85) 100%)`

## Typography
The system couples the geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the sharp, futuristic discipline of **Space Grotesk**.

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered exclusively in Plus Jakarta Sans 800 (ExtraBold) and 700 (Bold). Tight tracking creates punchy, comic-book-inspired titles that command attention without feeling aggressive.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: Utilizes regular and medium weights of Plus Jakarta Sans to maximize legibility across both dark glass overlays and opaque containers.
- **Labels, Trackers & Data**: Handled by Space Grotesk in uppercase with extended tracking. This creates a technical, studio-grade aesthetic for agency metrics, project meta labels, and floating badge counters.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column structural grid underpins wide viewports, tapering to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Rhythm & Proportions**:
  - Desktop (≥1200px): Max page wrapper width is `1360px`. Canvas margins are set to `margin` (3rem/48px), column gutters to `gutter` (1.5rem/24px).
  - Tablet (768px – 1199px): Margins scale to `2rem`, column gutters to `1.25rem`.
  - Mobile (<768px): Margins tighten to `margin-mobile` (1.25rem/20px) to maximize tactile touch-surface footprint.
- **Reflow & Hierarchy**:
  - Complex multi-column grid arrays compress to stacked modular cards on mobile.
  - Floating stickers, badges, and offset decorative accents anchor directly to parent card containers using relative coordinates, ensuring layout resilience across varying aspect ratios.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through two intersecting techniques: **Tactile Physical Extrusions** and **Bioluminescent Ambient Bloom**.

1. **Tactile Extrusion (The 3D "Stomp" Effect)**:
   - Interactive elements employ bottom solid ledges created via heavy directional box-shadows or solid bottom-borders (4px to 6px thick) rendered in the component's darker chromatic shade (`#009FA9` for Cyan, `#A30F63` for Magenta, `#5F2197` for Purple, `#1A1F2C` for neutral surfaces).
   - On hover, the element lifts by 1px with increased bounce.
   - On `:active`, the component translates downward (`translateY(4px)`), while the extrusion ledge collapses to 0px, simulating an authentic physical spring mechanism.

2. **Ambient Neon Blooms**:
   - Depth is not rendered via generic black dropshadows. Dark surfaces hover above blurred, neon-tinted radial glows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 50px -10px rgba(0, 217, 232, 0.2), 0 10px 30px -5px rgba(236, 22, 143, 0.25)`).

3. **Dark Glassmorphism**:
   - Surface cards use translucent backdrops (`rgba(26, 31, 44, 0.75)`) combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a sharp, ultra-fine 1px border gradient transitioning from white at 20% opacity at the top rim to cyan/magenta at 10% opacity at the base.

## Shapes
The design system embraces an exaggerated roundedness profile (`roundedness: 3`). 

- **Primary interactive elements (Buttons, Chips, Badges)**: Feature full pill geometries (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to reinforce the toy-like, clickable aesthetic.
- **Content Cards & Media Modules**: Use generous `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px) radii, giving layouts an inviting, pebble-smooth silhouette.
- **Inputs & Modals**: Maintain heavy rounding (20px to 24px), completely eliminating sharp corners throughout the entire experience.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Tactile 3D Pill (Primary Magenta)**:
  - Base: High-saturation magenta (`#EC168F`), white bold text, `rounded-full`, 16px vertical padding, 32px horizontal padding.
  - Extrusion: Solid bottom ridge `4px solid #A30F63` (or `box-shadow: 0 5px 0 0 #A30F63`).
  - Active: `transform: translateY(4px); box-shadow: 0 0 0 0 #A30F63;`.
- **Tactile 3D Pill (Primary Cyan)**:
  - Base: Electric cyan (`#00D9E8`), midnight ink text (`#090B0F`), `rounded-full`.
  - Extrusion: `box-shadow: 0 5px 0 0 #009FA9`.
  - Active: Translates downward by 4px on press.
- **Glass Secondary Button**:
  - Base: Surface dark (`#1A1F2C`), 1.5px border in `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`, white text, `rounded-full`.
  - Extrusion: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #090B0F`.

### Cards & Containers
- **Agency Showcase Card**:
  - Background: `rgba(26, 31, 44, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
  - Border: 1px continuous outline with an iridescent dual gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(0,217,232,0.4), rgba(236,22,143,0.4))`).
  - Radius: `32px`.
  - Elevation: Baseline subtle neon halo that expands dynamically on card hover.
- **Stats Counter Card**:
  - Background: `#121620`, thick bottom border (`4px solid #1A1F2C`).
  - Metric typography in display scale, accompanied by micro label in `Space Grotesk` uppercase.

### Chips, Stickers & Badges
- **Floating Sticker Badge**:
  - Tilted rotation (`transform: rotate(-3deg)` or `+4deg`), pill-shaped, rendered in either pure white (`#F7F7FA`) with black text, or hyper magenta.
  - Border: `2px solid #090B0F` or pure white drop ring for instant separation from dark cards.
- **Filter Chips**:
  - Unselected: Dark surface `#1A1F2C`, rounded-full, 1px border with `text-secondary`.
  - Selected: Bright cyan `#00D9E8` background, `#090B0F` text, bottom tactile ridge of 3px `#009FA9`.

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Text Input**:
  - Height: 56px, fully rounded (`rounded-2xl` or `rounded-full`), background `#121620`, border `2px solid #242B3D`.
  - Typography: `Plus Jakarta Sans` regular, placeholder in `#64748B`.
  - Focus State: Border snaps to `#00D9E8` with a soft electric blue ambient glow (`0 0 0 4px rgba(0, 217, 232, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Oversized (24px × 24px) rounded squares (`rounded-lg`) and circles.
  - Unchecked: `#1A1F2C` with thick `#242B3D` edge.
  - Checked: Spring-pop animation to `#EC168F` with a bold white check glyph and a 3px tactile bottom shadow.

### Additional Signature Components
- **Floating Gamified Pill Bar**: A persistent bottom dock or top nav encased in frosted glass with tactile mini-icon buttons and cyan-to-magenta glowing status indicators.
- **Hover Micro-Stickers**: Contextual floating tooltips structured like glossy peel-off vinyl stickers with bold text, dropped shadows, and slight rotation angles.
